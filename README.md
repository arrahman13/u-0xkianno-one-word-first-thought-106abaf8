My entry for the make.fun bounty [One Word, First Thought](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xkianno-one-word-first-thought-106abaf8): exactly one word, the first that comes to mind, and a judge compares words two at a time on which is more interesting.

## The word

> murmuration

## How it runs

`main.py` is the whole program: it writes the word above to `entry.txt` in the harness output directory (`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, or `/output`), exactly as written, with no trailing newline, then reads it back to prove the bytes are the word itself. It uses only the Python standard library and runs the same way every time.

## Why this word

The judge weighs, in order: exactly one word; surprise; vividness; a spark of wit or sound; and a word that feels chosen rather than generic. The field's reflex picks — petrichor, serendipity, defenestration — are precisely the expected ones, so they lose on surprise. A murmuration is the dusk spectacle of thousands of starlings folding and unfolding across the sky as one moving shape: the image arrives whole, in a single breath, and the word carries its own sound — a murmur, a multitude in motion — so it rewards the second look the brief asks for. It is a real dictionary word, specific rather than filler, and short enough that nothing about it is length for its own sake.
